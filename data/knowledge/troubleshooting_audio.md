# OmniTech AeroPulse ANC-X Headphones - User Manual & Troubleshooting

## 1. Product Specifications & Features
- **Driver**: 40mm Titanium Composite Dynamic Drivers with Active Noise Cancellation (Hybrid ANC up to -42dB).
- **Battery Life**: Up to 45 hours with ANC disabled; 32 hours with ANC active. Fast charging provides 5 hours of playback from a 10-minute charge via USB-C.
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth 5.3 with Multipoint support (connect up to 2 devices simultaneously), SBC, AAC, LDAC audio codecs.
- **Controls**: Capacitive touch gesture pad on right earcup, physical ANC toggle button, physical power/pairing button.

## 2. Bluetooth Pairing & Multipoint Setup
1. **Initial Pairing**: Turn the headphones off. Press and hold the Power button for 5 seconds until the LED flashes alternating Blue and Red, accompanied by voice prompt "Pairing Mode". Select "OmniTech AeroPulse ANC-X" on your phone or laptop.
2. **Multipoint Connection**: After pairing with Device A, turn off Bluetooth on Device A. Re-enter pairing mode on the headphones and connect Device B. Finally, re-enable Bluetooth on Device A and tap the headphone name in its Bluetooth menu. Both devices will now be connected simultaneously.

## 3. Common Troubleshooting & Hardware Reset
- **One Ear Silent or Audio Stutter**: Place headphones within 1 meter of source device. Ensure high-bandwidth 2.4GHz Wi-Fi interference is minimized. If audio persists in one ear only, perform a Factory Reset.
- **Factory Reset Procedure**: While headphones are powered on, simultaneously press and hold the Power button and ANC button for 8 seconds until the LED flashes purple 3 times. All saved paired devices will be erased.
- **ANC Ineffective or Whistling Noise**: Inspect the exterior feedforward mic grilles on both earcups for dirt, dust, or lint accumulation. Gently clean with a soft dry bristle brush. Ensure ear cushions form an airtight acoustic seal over ears.
