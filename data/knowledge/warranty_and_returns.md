# OmniTech Electronics - Warranty, Returns & Refund Policy

## 1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
All new hardware purchased directly from OmniTech Electronics comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee starting from the confirmed delivery date.
- Items must be in original condition with all accessories, cables, manuals, and original packaging.
- A 15% restocking fee applies if the product packaging has been unsealed or opened, except in cases where the product was delivered defective.
- Items returned damaged due to customer misuse, liquid damage, or unauthorized modifications are non-refundable.

## 2. Limited Warranty Coverage
All OmniTech branded products include a 1-year (12-month) limited hardware warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
- **Batteries**: Rechargeable lithium-ion batteries carry a separate 6-month limited warranty. Normal capacity degradation (loss of up to 20% capacity within 1 year) is considered normal wear and tear and is not covered.
- **Water Damage**: Water ingress is covered ONLY for devices certified with 5ATM or IP68 ratings when used strictly within user manual parameters. Hot water, sauna, steam, and saltwater exposure void the water resistance warranty immediately.
- **Repairs**: If a covered defect occurs within the warranty period, OmniTech will either repair the device with new/refurbished OEM parts or replace it with an equivalent model at zero service fee.

## 3. Return Procedure & Shipping Costs
1. Customers must obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number by submitting an online ticket or requesting support via the automated customer service portal.
2. Defective items returned within the first 14 days will receive a pre-paid DHL/Kerry Express return shipping label.
3. For change-of-mind returns after 14 days, the customer is responsible for the return shipping postage.
4. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned package is received and inspected at our central warehouse in Bangkok.
