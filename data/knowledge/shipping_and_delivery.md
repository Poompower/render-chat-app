# OmniTech Electronics - Shipping, Delivery & Tracking Guidelines

## 1. Shipping Methods & Timeframes
OmniTech operates fulfillment hubs in Bangkok and Samut Prakan. We partner with Flash Express, Kerry Express, and DHL eCommerce.
- **Standard Domestic Shipping**: Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days nationwide across Thailand. Free for all orders exceeding 1,500 THB; flat rate of 60 THB for smaller orders.
- **Express Same-Day / Next-Day Delivery**: Available exclusively in the Bangkok Metropolitan Region (BMA) for orders placed before 14:00. Flat rate fee of 150 THB.
- **International Shipping**: Available to select ASEAN countries (Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, Philippines). Estimated transit time is 5 to 10 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

## 2. Order Tracking & Status Definitions
Once an order is dispatched from our fulfillment facility, an automated SMS and email containing a 10-digit tracking number (e.g., `TH-KRY-XXXXX` or `TH-EXP-XXXXX`) is sent to the customer.
- **Processing**: Order has been paid and is being assembled and packed in the warehouse.
- **Shipped**: Package has been handed over to the courier service and is in transit.
- **Out for Delivery**: The local courier driver has received the parcel for final delivery today.
- **Delivered**: Parcel has been successfully handed over to recipient or left in a secure location.
- **Cancelled**: Order was cancelled prior to dispatch. Funds are automatically refunded within 24 hours.

## 3. Damaged or Lost Shipments
If a shipment arrives visibly damaged:
- Customers must take high-resolution photos of the outer box, courier label, and inner contents before discarding any packaging materials.
- Claims must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery status update.
- If tracking shows no transit movement for over 7 business days, OmniTech initiates an investigation with the carrier and will issue a free expedited replacement upon carrier confirmation of package loss.
