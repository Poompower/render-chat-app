# OmniTech ChronoFit Pro Smartwatch - User Manual & Troubleshooting

## 1. Device Overview & Sensor Specifications
- **Display**: 1.43-inch AMOLED display (466x466 resolution, 1000 nits peak brightness, Always-On Display supported).
- **Sensors**: Optical PPG Heart Rate & SpO2 sensor, 3-axis accelerometer, barometric altimeter, dual-frequency GPS (L1+L5).
- **Water Resistance**: 5ATM water rating (safe for recreational swimming in shallow water up to 50 meters static pressure). NOT suitable for diving, water skiing, hot tubs, saunas, or high-velocity water sports.
- **Battery**: 420mAh capacity. 10 days typical usage, 4 days with Always-On Display enabled, 22 hours continuous GPS tracking.

## 2. Sensor Accuracy & Heart Rate Calibration
- **Inaccurate Heart Rate Readings**: Wear the watch snugly approximately one finger's width above the wrist bone. Skin temperature, excessive wrist movement during weightlifting, tattoos, or loose straps cause inaccurate PPG readings. Clean the bottom optical glass with a damp lint-free cloth.
- **GPS Fix Taking Long**: Ensure an unobstructed view of the sky when initiating an outdoor activity. Sync the watch with the OmniFit mobile app daily to update Assisted-GPS (A-GPS) satellite ephemeris data.

## 3. Battery Drain & Connectivity Troubleshooting
- **Abnormal Battery Drain**: Disable Continuous 24/7 SpO2 tracking if not medically necessary. Set screen timeout to 5 seconds and lower wake-on-wrist gesture sensitivity. Check that watch face does not contain animated real-time widgets.
- **Notifications Not Arriving from iPhone or Android**: Open phone settings -> Permissions -> ensure "OmniFit App" has background execution and notification access enabled without aggressive OS battery optimization kill rules.
- **Hard Reboot & System Reset**: Press and hold the top rotating crown button for 12 seconds until the OmniTech logo appears on screen. For full factory reset, navigate on watch to: Settings -> System -> Reset -> Erase All Content.
