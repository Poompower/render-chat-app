# OmniTech Hardware Devices - TitanBook 14 Laptop & MechWave RGB Keyboard

## 1. TitanBook 14 Laptop Specifications & Thermal Management
- **Processor**: Intel Core Ultra 7 155H with integrated NPU and Intel Arc Graphics.
- **Display**: 14.5-inch 2.8K (2880x1800) OLED, 120Hz refresh rate, 100% DCI-P3 color gamut.
- **Ports & Charging**: 2x Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C 100W Power Delivery and DisplayPort 1.4 alt mode), 1x USB-A 3.2 Gen 2, 1x HDMI 2.1, 3.5mm combo audio jack.
- **Thermal Management & Fan Profiles**: Press `Fn + Q` to cycle between Quiet (15W TDP), Balanced (28W TDP), and Extreme Performance (45W TDP) modes. To prevent thermal throttling, ensure the bottom intake grilles and rear exhaust vents are clear from soft surfaces like beds or carpets.

## 2. TitanBook Display & External Monitors
- **Dual External Monitor Setup**: The laptop supports up to two 4K 60Hz external monitors simultaneously through the Thunderbolt 4 ports or one 4K 120Hz display via HDMI 2.1.
- **USB-C Charging Requirement**: Requires a minimum 65W Power Delivery charger to charge while operating; using 45W chargers will result in slow charging and "Plugged in, not charging" warnings under heavy CPU loads.

## 3. MechWave RGB Mechanical Keyboard Troubleshooting
- **Switch Specifications**: Hot-swappable 5-pin Gateron Pro switches (Red Linear or Brown Tactile). Keycaps are double-shot PBT OEM profile.
- **Triple-Mode Connection**: Switch the toggle switch on the back to 2.4G (Wireless USB Dongle), BT (Bluetooth 5.0 up to 3 devices), or Cable (USB-C Wired).
- **Key Chatter or Unresponsive Keys**: Mechanical key switches are hot-swappable. Use the included metal switch puller to gently grasp the top and bottom clips of the affected switch and pull vertically upwards. Clean the socket contacts with compressed air and re-insert the switch ensuring pins are straight.
- **Bluetooth Re-pairing**: Long press `Fn + 1`, `Fn + 2`, or `Fn + 3` for 3 seconds until the corresponding indicator LED blinks rapidly to enter pairing mode.
